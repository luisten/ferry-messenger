# Ferry Messenger privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Ferry Messenger is an app for your Instagram direct messages. It is made by one person, has no server, and collects
nothing about you.

## What the app does with your data

- **Your login.** You log in on Instagram's own login page, inside the app. The app never sees or stores your
  password.
- **Your Instagram session** stays in your iPhone's Keychain, on your iPhone only. Removing an account in the app
  deletes it from the Keychain.
- **Your messages, photos and videos** go between your iPhone and Instagram only. The app keeps a copy of recent
  media on your iPhone so chats open quickly; iOS can clear it at any time, and deleting the app deletes it.
- **Your settings** (account names and colours, archived chats) stay on your iPhone.

## Who the app talks to

- **Instagram**, to show and send your messages. What Instagram does with them is up to Instagram's own privacy
  policy.
- **The address of a GIF** you send as a link, to download that GIF and send it as a picture.
- **GitHub, only if you built the app yourself from its source code:** once a day, to see whether there is a new
  version (you can turn this off in Accounts). It sends nothing about you or your accounts. The App Store version
  never does this.

The developer receives nothing: no analytics, no crash reports, no tracking, no advertising, and nothing is sold or
shared.

## Children

Instagram is for people 13 and older, and so is this app.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are dated above and kept in the app's public source code. Questions: open an issue at
<https://github.com/luisten/ferry-messenger/issues>.

Ferry Messenger is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
