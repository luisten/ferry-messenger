Why it happened

  • Tiles are permanent and sized for content that's usually absent: icon, count and label take at least 62 pt (DESIGN said 52; they render at 65), drawn even at zero.
  • iOS 26 moved search to the bottom bar. The space under the large title used to hold the search field. The trailing filter menu was cut in M3, so the bar holds one button.
  • The large title and the chips both say where you are.
  • Accessibility sizes: tiles stack into 120 pt rows. Two take 250 pt (first chat at y 534); with General, about 370 pt.
  • Scrolled, it's fine (164 pt): the title collapses and the tiles scroll off. But an inbox is at rest at the top almost every time.

Not viable for Requests: the brief requires them to stay clearly separate from Chats. For General, removal discards the Primary/General split the bridge patch (D12) bought. For Archived it's workable: search finds archived chats, and they come back on a new message.

3 · The top of the screen

These frames keep today's tiles so only the header changes. Inline title: yes. Merging the account stack into the chips: no.

4 · Recommended: inline title, Folders menu, request card

H1 + H2 + B, plus a slim Requests card shown only while there are requests you haven't looked at yet. General stays quiet (badge and menu), as it does on Instagram. Archived lives in the menu, and search still finds archived chats. Folders are still places: each item pushes today's folder screen, so request privacy is unchanged.

Rules

  • Badge = new requests in scope + unread General chats in scope. Label color; hidden at 0.
  • Card: shown while the scope has requests newer than the last time Requests was opened there. That marker is a local timestamp; nothing is sent.
  • Menu: General only where a professional account is in scope (today's showsGeneral). Subtitles only when non-zero; never a bare "0".
  • Zero: nothing at all between the chips and the first chat.
  • AX sizes: no tiles to stack. The card grows with its text (about 90 pt, and only when present).
  • VoiceOver: "Folders, 2 requests waiting". Card: "2 message requests, from Lena Ortiz and crypto.wins.daily. Opens Requests."
