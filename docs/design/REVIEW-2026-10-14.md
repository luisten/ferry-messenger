# Design review: inbox density (v1.1)

2026-10-14. This is a review pass on the shipped v1.1, not a redesign. It responds to the user's note: "the general,
requests and archive tray take up too much space. And there's also a lot of empty space at the top."
The mockups are in [`mockups-v2.html`](mockups-v2.html). Inputs: [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md), [DECISIONS](../DECISIONS.md)
D5/D9/D10/D12/D13, [specs.md](../../specs.md), [m7-design-diff.md](../verification/m7-design-diff.md),
`InboxView.swift`, `RootView.swift`.

**Captures.** New captures were taken on the iPhone 17 simulator (iOS 26.4, 402 × 874 pt, light) at 07:40–07:44:
- the All scope and both account scopes (here: Alex, and Alex Studio, the professional one);
- All again, relaunched with `IGM_DEBUG_REQUESTS=1` (three fake requests).

They aren't in the repo, because they show real accounts and `docs/verification/*.png` is gitignored. Their geometry
matches `docs/verification/m8-inbox-*.png` (one per scope) exactly, so those files are the reference
images here. Every measurement comes from a full-size screenshot (pixels ÷ 3), using row and column scans, so values are
accurate to about 1 pt. The accessibility-size reference is `m7a-inbox-all-ax3.png`, taken before M8, so it has two
tiles. No chat was opened and nothing was typed. Afterwards the app was relaunched with no environment variables, so it
runs on the user's Keychain session again.

## Summary

- **242 pt of chrome before the first chat.** Between the status bar and the first chat sit 242 pt of chrome: 28% of
  the screen, or 3.2 chat rows.
  - **92 pt are the folder tiles.** On a normal day they read "0 · 0 · (blank)".
  - **106 pt are the navigation bar and the large title.** The bar holds one 75 pt button. The title repeats the chip
    selected just below it.
- **Recommendation.**
  - The title becomes inline.
  - The folders move into a **Folders menu**, placed in the top-right slot that is empty today. A badge sits on it.
  - A slim **Requests card** appears at the top of the list only while there are requests you haven't looked at.
  - Result: the first chat moves from y 304 to y 164, and 8.5 rows are visible instead of 6.7. The folders are still
    places; only the tiles go. Cost: **S**.
- **Other screens.** Two medium findings: the thread header truncates the account name, and the Save bar on O5 hides
  the color picker. There are also five low findings (§d).

## (a) What's wasting space in the inbox header

### Measured: All scope, iPhone 17 (402 × 874 pt)

| Band | y (pt) | Height | What's there | What it tells you on a typical day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Status bar + Dynamic Island | 0–62 | 62 | system | fixed; not ours to spend |
| Navigation bar | 62–116 | 54 | Account stack, a 75 × 44 glass capsule at x 16–91. **The other 295 pt of the bar are empty.** | Accounts entry and health |
| Large title | 116–168 | 52 | "Chats" (glyphs 128–153) | Nothing the "All" chip under it doesn't say |
| Scope chips | 168–212 | 44 | 34 pt capsules at 174–208, 44 pt touch targets | The one control |
| Folder tiles | 212–304 | **92** | Three 65 pt tiles (231–296), 19 pt of space above and 8 pt below | Usually nothing: "0", "0" and a blank |
| First chat row | from 304 | 76 per row | avatar 314–366 | |

- **Chrome below the status bar:** 242 pt, which is 3.2 chat rows (rows are 76 pt apart). The first chat starts 35%
  of the way down the screen.
- **Rows visible above the search bar** (which starts at 811): 6.7.
- **Account scopes** have the same geometry. In the Alex Studio scope, 304 pt of header sit above the one chat that
  account has today (`m8-inbox-*`). The title block uses the same 52 pt, but it moves up to fit the subtitle. The
  account name's glyphs start at y 107, and the account stack capsule ends at y 106.
- **Repetition.** In an account scope, the name appears three times in the top 220 pt: in the title, on the filled
  chip, and in the search placeholder ("Search Alex Studio"). The wash and the tint also signal the account.
- **Zeros are the boldest numbers on screen.** The tile counts use `.headline.weight(.bold)`. With three requests
  waiting (DEBUG capture), the Requests tile shows "3" in the same weight and place as the General tile's "0". Waiting
  requests therefore don't stand out at all, which defeats the reason the count is there.
- **At accessibility sizes it gets much worse.** The tiles stack into full-width rows of about 120 pt each. Two tiles
  take 250 pt (`m7a-inbox-all-ax3`), and the first chat starts at y 534, 61% of the way down. With General's tile, three
  tiles would take about 370 pt, leaving room for roughly one chat above the search bar.
- **Scrolled down, it's fine.** The large title collapses into the bar, the tiles scroll away, and the chips stay
  pinned, so the chrome shrinks to 62 + 54 + 48 = 164 pt. The problem is the resting state. An inbox sorted newest
  first sits at rest at the top almost every time you open it.

### Why

1. **Tiles are permanent, and sized for content that's usually absent.** Each tile needs an icon, a count and a label
   (at least 62 pt; DESIGN §8.4 specified 52). They're drawn even at zero. DESIGN §4.2 wanted the request count
   "always visible" so that requests stay out of sight but known. At zero, that rule draws a zero three times.
2. **iOS 26 moved search to the bottom.** In iOS 18 the space under the large title held the search field. Now the large
   title sits alone above a bar that holds one button. The trailing filter menu (DESIGN I1) was cut in M3, so the
   right side of the bar is empty.
3. **Double labelling.** The large title and the scope bar both say where you are. DESIGN chose the large title before
   the scope bar became the main way to orient yourself.
4. **The tiles look like a second control row.** Two stacked rows of rounded shapes (chips, then tiles) read as two
   controls. That weakens "one scope control" even though the tiles are navigation.
5. **The large title also forced a workaround.** `safeAreaBar(edge: .top)` under a large title pulls the title into
   the scroll-edge blur (CLAUDE.md), so the chips sit in `safeAreaInset` instead. They lose the iOS 26 scroll-edge
   effect.

    > **Could not verify:** the list is too short to scroll, so I couldn't confirm whether rows show through behind the
    > chips once the title collapses.

## (b) Options for General, Requests and Archived

The constraints, from the brief and DESIGN:
- Requests must stay clearly separate from Chats (opening one never sends "Seen").
- General exists only for professional accounts (`isProfessional`, D12) and fills only when Instagram reports a move
  (D9).
- Archived is user-driven.
- On a typical day, with 2–4 accounts, all three are empty or nearly empty.

**Cost scale:**
- **S:** contained in `InboxView.swift`, roughly 100 lines or fewer; `InboxRoute.folder` and `FolderView` stay as they
  are.
- **M:** also changes the `Inbox` model, list state, several views or the UI tests in a non-trivial way.
- **L:** new screens or new data.

### Option A: compact pills, shown only when a folder has something

- **Looks:** the tile row becomes a row of 32 pt capsules under the chips, e.g. `[?] Requests 2` and `[tray] General 1`.
  Nothing is drawn when there's nothing to show. Archived has no pill, because it would be permanent; it becomes a
  plain "Archived" row after the last chat.
- **Reach:**
  - Requests: tap its pill.
  - General: tap its pill. It shows whenever General holds any chats, with a count only when some are unread.
  - Archived: scroll to the end.
- **Noticing:** the pill appearing is the signal (slide in, `Motion.standard`), and it carries a count.
- **Zero:** 0 pt.
- **Cost:** S. `FolderTiles` becomes a filtered `FolderPills` with a new style; add one end-of-list row.
- **Trade-offs:**
  - The list jumps 44 pt down when the first request arrives.
  - Folders get two homes (pills and the end row).
  - A professional account that uses General keeps its pill permanently. That's fine, since it's real content.

### Option B: a Folders menu in the toolbar

- **Looks:** a 44 pt glass button in the empty top-right slot of the navigation bar (glyph `tray.2`), with a
  monochrome badge. It opens a menu:
  - General, with "1 unread" (only in scopes that include a professional account, as `Inbox.showsGeneral` decides
    today);
  - Requests, with "2 waiting";
  - Archived.
- **Reach:** two taps for each folder (button, then item). Each item pushes the same `FolderView` as today.
- **Noticing:** the badge only. It's small, and easy to miss for requests.
- **Zero:** 0 pt. The button has no badge and the items have no numbers.
- **Cost:** S, about 30 lines; delete `FolderTiles`. Apple's Messages app takes the same approach in iOS 26: unknown
  senders and spam sit behind its top-right filter menu.

### Option C: rows at the end of the list, like Mail and Signal, plus a banner row for requests

- **Looks:**
  - Three plain rows after the last chat: General, Requests and Archived, each with its glyph and count.
  - A "2 message requests" row at the top of the list, only while requests exist.
- **Reach:**
  - From the top banner row when it's there.
  - Otherwise, scroll to the end of the list.
- **Noticing:** the top banner row.
- **Zero:** the end rows stay. They take 132 pt at the bottom: visible on short lists like today's, off-screen on long
  ones.
- **Cost:** S.
- **Trade-offs:**
  - General is hard to reach in a busy professional scope.
  - The end of the list is an odd place for something that isn't an afterthought.

### Option D: General as a sub-filter in a professional account's scope

- **Looks:** in Alex Studio's scope, the title becomes a title menu: "Alex Studio ⌄ / @alexstudio · Primary". Choosing
  General swaps the list in place, and the subtitle changes to "· General". One variant uses a `Primary | General`
  segmented control under the chips, in that scope only (44 pt).
- **Reach:** tap the title.
- **Noticing:** a count in the menu ("General · 1 unread").
- **Zero:** nothing changes.
- **Cost:** M. The main list needs folder state (`@SceneStorage`), empty states and search per folder. In All scope,
  General would either be dropped or need its own title menu.
- **Trade-offs:**
  - It covers General only; Requests and Archived still need A, B or C.
  - It changes "folders are places" for General: General becomes a filter. This is closest to Instagram's own tabs.
    It's worth it only if the user lives in General.

### Option E: remove the folders entirely

| Folder | What removal breaks |
|---|---|
| Requests | **Not viable.** Requests would mix with chats: spam lands in the list, Accept and Delete need a place, and the "they won't see that you've read this" cue loses its screen. The brief requires clear separation. Burying them in Accounts makes time-sensitive requests invisible. |
| General | Folding General back into the main list throws away the Primary/General split that M8 and D12 built: the bridge patch, the subtitle, and the chip counts that exclude General. The only thing left would be a "General" tag on rows. |
| Archived | Workable. Archived chats are still found by search (I5) and come back when a new message arrives. But nothing says where an archived chat went, and the "All caught up / your other chats are in Archived" copy loses its target. |

- **Cost:** S to delete, but Requests still need A, B or C.
- **Useful part:** Archived doesn't deserve a permanent slot.

## (c) Recommendation

**H1 + H2 + B, plus the Requests card from C (top row only, only while there are new requests).** General stays quiet
(badge and menu). Archived lives in the menu, and search keeps finding archived chats.

### What it looks like (mockups-v2 "Recommended")

- **Navigation bar (62–116):**
  - Leading: the account stack, unchanged.
  - Centre: an inline title. "Chats" in All; in an account scope, the account name with the existing subtitle ("@handle",
    or "@handle · Primary" for a professional account).
  - Trailing: the **Folders** button (glass, `tray.2`) with a badge, opening a `Menu`. Its badge counts the new
    requests and unread General chats in scope, uses label color (monochrome chrome), and is hidden at zero.
- **Folders menu:** General `tray.full` "1 unread" (scopes with a professional account only), Requests
  `person.crop.circle.badge.questionmark` "2 waiting", and Archived `archivebox`. Subtitles appear only when non-zero.
  Each item pushes `.folder(folder, scope)` as the tiles do today, so `FolderView` and its request rules (Accept,
  Delete, no receipts) don't change.
- **Scope chips (116–160):** unchanged, pinned, with the scroll-edge effect.
- **Requests card:** the first list row, shown only while the scope has requests newer than the last time you opened
  Requests in that scope.
  - **The marker is local:** a timestamp per scope in `UserDefaults`. Nothing is sent, and `m.read.private` stays the
    user's call (CLAUDE.md).
  - **Content:**
    - leading: a 36 pt circle with the requests glyph;
    - "2 message requests";
    - the requester names (in All with 2+ accounts, each with its account mark);
    - trailing: a chevron.
  - **Style:** a filled card (`tertiarySystemFill`, radius 16, about 56 pt), deliberately unlike a chat row. It shows no
    message text, so spam never reaches the main list.
  - **Behaviour:** it pushes the Requests list, never a request thread.
  - **VoiceOver:** "2 message requests, from Lena Ortiz and crypto.wins.daily. Opens Requests."
- **General:** no card. Instagram treats General as the quieter tab (as far as I know it doesn't notify for General by
  default), so unread General chats only feed the badge and the menu count. In a professional account's scope, the
  subtitle "· Primary" keeps saying that this list isn't everything.
- **Zero state:**
  - No card, no badge. Between the chips and the first chat there's nothing.
  - The menu is still one tap away, and the empty folder screens are unchanged.
- **Accessibility sizes:** no tiles to stack. The chips already scroll, and the card grows with its text (about 90 pt
  at AX3, and only when present).

**Geometry (iPhone 17):**

| State | First chat at | Rows visible | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical day | y ≈ 164 (was 304) | 8.5 (was 6.7) | −140 pt, about 1.8 more rows |
| With the Requests card | y ≈ 228 | 7.7 | still 76 pt better than today |

### Why, against the principles

- **Color means account: unchanged.** The badge and the card are monochrome. No status colors are used for counts.
- **One control picks whose chats you see: strengthened.** The chips are now the only thing between the title and the
  list. The second row of controls is gone.
- **Folders are places, not filters: kept.** Each folder still pushes its own screen with its own rules. That matters
  most for Requests: separation and "nothing is sent" stay exactly as built, and the card is visibly not a chat.
- **Learn account facts from data: unchanged.** General appears only where `isProfessional` (or an actual General
  chat) says so.
- **Native first: improved.** An inline title, a toolbar `Menu`, a badge and a list row are all stock. One custom view
  (`FolderTiles` with its AX layout) is deleted.
- **One engineer can build it:** S.

### Principle and spec changes (explicit)

| Where | Was | Becomes |
|---|---|---|
| DESIGN §0.4 | "Folders (Requests, later General, plus Archived) are tiles at the top of the list that push a screen" | "Folders live in the **Folders menu** (top right) and push a screen scoped to the current selection. A folder shows itself in the list only when it has something new: today, that's new requests." The first half ("one control… folders are places, not filters") stays. |
| DESIGN §4.2 | "Requests stay out of sight, but the count is always visible." | "…the count is visible whenever it isn't zero (badge, menu, and a card for new requests)." Zero is no longer drawn. |
| DESIGN §4.1, I1 | Large title "Chats" / account name | Inline title; same text and subtitle. |
| DESIGN §4.5 | "Requests tile: unread requests in scope" | "Folders badge: new requests (since you last opened Requests in this scope) plus unread General chats." |
| DESIGN §8.4 | `Size.tileHeight` 52 | removed |

### Build notes (InboxView.swift, about 100 lines changed, mostly deleted)

1. `.navigationBarTitleDisplayMode(.inline)` on `InboxView`. Keep `navigationTitle` and `navigationSubtitle`.
2. Replace `FolderTiles` with a `ToolbarItem(placement: .topBarTrailing)`:
   - a `Menu` of three `Button`s calling the existing `open(.folder(…))`;
   - `accessibilityIdentifier("inbox.folders")` on the button;
   - `folder.<name>` kept on the items.
3. Add a `RequestsCard` as the first `List` row, using `Inbox.rooms(…, folder: .requests)`. Store the "last opened"
   timestamp when `FolderView(.requests)` appears.
4. Move `ScopeBar` from `safeAreaInset` to `safeAreaBar(edge: .top)`. Lower `AccountWash` from 260 to about 200 pt.
5. Copy: the empty-state lines "Your other chats are in General / Archived" gain "Open Folders, top right".
6. `SmokeUITests`: two call sites tap `folder.requests`. Tap `inbox.folders` first.
7. Update DESIGN.md (table above) and specs.md's Inbox section.

### Verify while building (I'm not certain of these)

- **Subtitle in the inline bar.** iOS 26 should show `navigationSubtitle` under an inline title. Check that
  "@alexstudio · Primary" fits beside a 75 pt stack and a 44 pt button. If it doesn't, drop "@handle" there; Chat Info
  and Accounts show it.
- **Toolbar badge.** I believe iOS 26 added `.badge(_:)` for toolbar items. If it can't be monochrome (a system red
  badge would break "color means account"), draw a small label-colored capsule as an overlay.
- **The chips trap.** CLAUDE.md records chips drawn about 128 pt too low when a horizontal `ScrollView` exists at the
  first layout. My hypothesis is that the large title's height causes it, so H1 may remove the trap and make H2 safe.
  Test right after onboarding, the case that triggered it.

## Smells noticed (separate issues, not changed)

- **Token drift.** DESIGN §8.4 says tiles are 52 pt; the code sets `minHeight: 62`, and the tiles render at 65.
- **General's tile count is unread-only.** A General folder full of read chats shows "0", which reads as "empty". The
  menu subtitle should say "1 unread" or nothing, never "0".
- **Request "unread" never clears, by design:** no receipts are sent in requests. So "new requests" can't come from
  Synapse's notification counts, and the card needs the local marker above.
- **Chips drawn about 128 pt low on first layout** (the CLAUDE.md trap). This is a workaround for a layout bug that
  the large title probably causes; see "Verify while building".

## Questions for the user

1. **Requests card:** hide it once you've looked at the Requests list (proposed; it uses a local marker and sends
   nothing), or keep it until you accept or delete the requests?
2. **General:** keep it quiet (badge and menu only, like Instagram), or give it a card too when it has unread chats?
3. **Inline title:** OK to drop the large "Chats" title? And is "Folders" with a tray icon the right label for the
   menu, or would you prefer the original filter glyph (`line.3.horizontal.decrease`) so Unread-only can join it later?
